ProgressBars renders quantised utilisation as a row of bars — one bar per unit of capacity (the ceiling of the limit) — filled left to right by the usage. The bar that straddles the usage boundary is partially filled ("blocked out") to show the fractional unit in use, and the trailing bars stay empty to show headroom; the bar colour crosses warning and danger thresholds. A "<used> / <capacity>" caption sits below with the icon+title. It is purely presentational — pass plain `usage`/`max` numbers (TimeseriesCoreBars wraps it with live data).
